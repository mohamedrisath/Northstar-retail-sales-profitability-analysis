# Business Recommendations

## 1. Tighten Furniture Discount Controls

Furniture profitability declined sharply as discount levels increased.

- No Discount: 22.86% profit margin
- 1–10% Discount: 15.21%
- 11–20% Discount: 3.20%
- 21–30% Discount: -10.77%
- 31–40% Discount: -30.91%
- Above 40% Discount: -70.84%

### Recommended Action

Introduce approval controls for Furniture discounts above approximately 20%.

Management should monitor whether lower discount levels can maintain sales volume while protecting profitability.

---

## 2. Review Tables and Bookcases Pricing

Tables generated approximately **-$17.8K profit**, while Bookcases generated approximately **-$3.6K**.

### Recommended Action

Review:

- Product pricing
- Supplier costs
- Promotional strategy
- Product-level margins
- Discount policies

for Tables and Bookcases.

---

## 3. Prioritise the East Region

Tables in the East region generated approximately **-$11.1K profit** and had an average discount of approximately **35.9%**.

This was the weakest regional Tables performance.

### Recommended Action

Audit discounting and pricing practices in the East region and compare them with the West region, where Tables remained slightly profitable.

---

## 4. Investigate Central Region Margin Leakage

Central generated approximately **$503K in sales**, but its regional profit margin was only around **7.92%**, the lowest of all four regions.

### Recommended Action

Investigate:

- Product mix
- Discount levels
- Loss-making products
- Category profitability

to determine why sales are not converting into stronger profit.

---

## 5. Protect High-Margin Categories

Technology and Office Supplies generated significantly stronger profitability than Furniture.

### Recommended Action

Continue supporting high-performing products through:

- Inventory availability
- Targeted marketing
- Controlled discounting
- Product prioritisation

while monitoring category-level margins.

---

## 6. Use Growth Trends for Business Planning

Sales growth reached approximately:

- **29.80% in 2025**
- **21.44% in 2026**

The business also experienced stronger sales periods toward the later months of the year.

### Recommended Action

Use historical growth and seasonal demand patterns to improve:

- Inventory planning
- Staffing
- Promotional timing
- Product availability

---

# Management Summary

The analysis indicates that Northstar Retail is experiencing strong overall business growth, but profitability is uneven across products and regions.

Furniture represents the largest profitability concern, particularly Tables and Bookcases. High discount levels are strongly associated with declining margins, while the East and Central regions require further management attention.

The recommended strategy is to strengthen discount governance, review loss-making product pricing, investigate regional margin leakage, and continue supporting high-performing categories.
